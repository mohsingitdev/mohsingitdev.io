Questions clients ask

What does a forward-deployed AI engineer do?

A forward-deployed AI engineer works inside a client team to take an AI system from prototype to production. That covers scoping the use case, designing the architecture, building the agents and retrieval, adding evals and guardrails, deploying in the client cloud, and handing over code and runbooks the team can run.

We built a prototype with Claude or another AI tool. Can you make it production-ready?

Yes. Prototypes built with Claude, Cursor, Lovable or similar tools usually prove the idea but miss authentication, data handling, guardrails, evaluation, monitoring and cost limits. I review what you have, keep what works, and rebuild the parts that will not hold up with real users. A readiness audit shows which is which in 1 to 2 weeks.

We are an early-stage startup. Why not hire a full-time AI engineer?

A senior AI hire usually takes months and adds a large fixed cost. An engagement gets the first production version live while you hire, and the handover documentation makes that hire productive from the first week.

Can you work as a vendor or contractor through our procurement?

Yes. I have delivered as a vendor and as a contract hire inside large organisations. I can work under your MSA, NDA and security questionnaires, or contract through the platform or agency you already use.

How long does it take to move an AI pilot to production?

For one well-scoped use case, usually 4 to 8 weeks: about a week to diagnose and agree a success metric, then design, build behind evaluation gates and a staged rollout. A readiness audit of an existing system takes 1 to 2 weeks.

Do you work with US, UK and Canadian teams from India?

Yes. I have delivered fully remote for US, UK and European clients since 2021. Daily overlap with US, UK or Canadian working hours is agreed at the start of each engagement.

Will you work alongside our in-house engineers?

Yes, that is the default. I pair with your team, review their code, document design decisions, and leave runbooks so your engineers own the system after handover.

I sign an NDA and an IP assignment before work starts and work inside your cloud, repositories and tools, so your data stays in your environment. Client work is only described publicly at the level the contract allows.

What do you need from us to start?

A named business owner, access to the relevant data and systems, and one success metric. A kickoff checklist covers accounts, environments and stakeholders in the first week.

What happens after handover?

You get the code in your repositories, architecture documents, runbooks, dashboards and the evaluation set. Ongoing support or an embedded retainer is available but not required.
