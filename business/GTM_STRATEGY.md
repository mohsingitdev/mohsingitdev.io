# Go-to-market plan: AI consulting, FDE and contract AI engineering

Owner: Mohsin Qureshi · Updated November 2026 · Website: https://mohsinqureshi.io/

## 1. Positioning (use everywhere, word for word)

**One line:** I turn stalled AI pilots into production systems that lower operating cost and give teams hours back.

**Two lines (bios, platform profiles):** Senior AI engineer and forward-deployed consultant. 5+ years shipping ML and GenAI to production for e-commerce, pharma, telecom and manufacturing clients; agents, RAG, model operations and revenue automation, remote with US and UK hours overlap.

**Proof points (only these, all verifiable):** document discovery hours → minutes; 40% shorter release cycles; 50% less manual oversight; 90%+ entity extraction accuracy; multi-agent platform taken from dev to production.

**Role titles to target (match job-board search terms):** Forward Deployed Engineer · AI Engineer (LLM / RAG / Agents) · LLMOps / MLOps Engineer · GTM Engineer · AI Automation Consultant · Applied AI Consultant.

## 2. The funnel

```
Channel (LinkedIn, recruiter, platform, forum)
  → Website: hero diagnostic (3 questions) or problems section
  → Enquiry form (email, prefilled) or LinkedIn DM
  → 15-minute call (qualify: problem, owner, timeline, budget range)
  → Written proposal within 48 hours (one metric, fixed scope)
  → Entry engagement: strategy session or readiness audit
  → Build or embedded retainer
```

Rules: every post, profile and message links to the website, not to a CV. The CV goes out only after a recruiter or client asks. Prices stay off the website; use the ranges in section 8 in proposals.

## 3. Channel playbook

### A. LinkedIn (primary channel, 60% of effort)

Profile:

- **Headline:** `AI Engineer & Forward-Deployed Consultant · Stalled AI pilots → production · Agents, RAG, LLMOps, GTM automation · US/UK remote`
- **About:** positioning line, the four proof points, "How I work" (diagnose → plan → build → hand over), call to action to the website diagnostic.
- **Featured:** website, the diagnostic (link to `/#top`), two reference architectures (`/case-studies`), one long-form post.
- **Open to:** Freelance and contract (Services page on LinkedIn with the five engagements, no prices).
- **Banner:** "Stalled AI pilots, shipped to production" + website URL.
- **Recommendations:** ask 5 former managers or client counterparts for a 3-sentence recommendation on one specific outcome. These become the testimonials you add to the site later.

Content (3 posts a week, rotate the five problems from the website):

| Day | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Problem teardown | "Why 86% of agent pilots don't scale, and the one metric that fixes it" |
| Wed | Build note / diagram | Routing architecture that keeps routine traffic off frontier models |
| Fri | Checklist or opinion | "7 questions before your RAG system goes to users" |

Each post ends with one line: "Free 3-question diagnosis on my site" + link in first comment.

Outreach (daily, 30 minutes):

- 15 connection requests to Heads of AI, CTOs, VP Engineering, RevOps leads at Series A–C US/UK companies that posted AI or FDE roles in the last 30 days (search Jobs → "Forward Deployed Engineer", "AI Engineer", then the hiring company's leaders).
- 10 thoughtful comments on posts by those buyers.
- Follow up accepted connections after 3 days (script in section 5).

### B. Recruiters and staffing partners (15%)

- Build a list of 30 recruiters who post AI/ML contract roles for US/UK (LinkedIn search: "AI recruiter contract", "ML engineering recruiter UK", agencies specialising in data and AI).
- Send the recruiter script, the website link and a one-page profile (PDF of the hero, proof points and role titles).
- Accept C2C/contractor roles via EOR or direct invoicing; state rate expectations privately, never on the site.

### C. Vetted talent platforms (15%)

| Platform | Why | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Toptal | Senior vetted, US clients | Long screening; client-side markup is high, so your rate must reflect it |
| Arc.dev | Remote-first senior roles, startups | Good for contract-to-hire |

Use the same headline, proof points and website link on every profile. Apply to 5 roles a week across platforms.

### D. Communities and forums (10%)

- **Hacker News**: post in "Who wants to be hired?" on the first weekday of each month (script below). Answer "Ask HN" threads on RAG, evals, LLM cost.
- **MLOps Community Slack, LangChain / LlamaIndex Discords, Latent Space Discord**: help in #help channels; use #jobs / #hiring only with the short bio.
- **Reddit**: r/LocalLLaMA, r/MachineLearning (no self-promotion; answer with depth), r/forhire for small first engagements.
- **GitHub**: keep two reference repos polished (`citation-grounded-rag`, `enterprise-agentic-router`) with a README that links to the site.

## 6. Discovery call (15 minutes)

1. What's the workflow and who owns the outcome?
2. What exists today (pilot, data, stack)?
3. What does success look like in one number?
4. Deadline and budget range?
5. Close: "I'll send a one-page proposal with scope, timeline and price within 48 hours."

## 7. Proposal template (one page)

Problem → success metric → scope (in / out) → plan by week → deliverables → price and payment terms (USD/GBP, 50% upfront for fixed scope) → start date.

## 8. Private rate guidance (for proposals only, not the website)

Based on 2026 market data for senior RAG/agent contractors (US direct roughly $150–250/hr; platforms lower) and published fixed-fee audits ($2,500–8,500):

| Engagement | Suggested range |
|---|---|
| Strategy session (60–90 min + notes) | $150–300 |
| Architecture review | $400–800 |
| Readiness audit (1–2 weeks) | $3,000–6,000 |
| Pilot to production (4–8 weeks) | $12,000–35,000 |
| Embedded, part-time (~20 hrs/week) | $6,000–9,000 / month |
| Hourly contract (platforms) | $70–120 / hr |

Raise rates by about 20% after each two delivered engagements with references.
