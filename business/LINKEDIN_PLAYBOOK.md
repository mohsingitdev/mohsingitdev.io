**Weekly:** follow up after 4–5 days once, then move on; update the pipeline sheet.

## 9. Automation (safe ways to do more with less time)

**Do not automate on LinkedIn itself.** Auto-connect, auto-DM and scraping tools (browser extensions and "growth" bots) break LinkedIn's User Agreement and regularly lead to restrictions or bans. Keep every send manual.

**What to automate:**

| Task | How | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| New matching roles | LinkedIn job alerts (native) → Gmail filter/label "Jobs: AI" | 10 min |
| Roles from outside LinkedIn | n8n or Zapier: RSS from HN "Who is hiring" (hnrss.org), We Work Remotely, RemoteOK → keyword filter (FDE, RAG, LLMOps) → daily email digest | 1 hr |
| Weekly market digest | A scheduled Claude task that searches the web for new remote FDE / AI contract roles and summarises the best 10 | 5 min (I can set this up) |
| Post drafting | Once a month, draft 12 posts with Claude from this playbook; edit and schedule with LinkedIn's built-in scheduler | 2 hrs / month |
| Pipeline CRM | Google Sheet or Notion: contact, company, role, stage, next step, date | 20 min |
| Enquiries into the CRM | Gmail filter on "Enquiry:" subject (from the website form) → Zapier → new CRM row + Slack/phone notification | 30 min |
| Calls into the CRM | Calendly → Zapier → CRM row + reminder to send the proposal within 48 hours | 20 min |
| Follow-up reminders | CRM "next step date" → daily email of due follow-ups | 15 min |
